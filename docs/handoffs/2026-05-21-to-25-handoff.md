## Decisions

- **Daily-recording dedup: UTC-safe, first-write-wins.** No unique constraint on
  (source,ticker,date) — check-then-insert in Python, dates compared in UTC.
- **IV data is NOT to be mass-backfilled.** IV is NULL for illiquid/deep-OTM/far-dated contracts
  by design (ThetaData rejects iv_error>50). 93% NULL overall, but that's the illiquid tail we
  DON'T want. The liquid near-the-money chain HAS IV (SPY: 9-13K liquid-ATM-IV rows/month, 12+
  months — well past the 500-gate). Fix is FILTERING, not backfill.
- **Vol-skew signal codegen must filter by DELTA band**, not fixed moneyness. Delta jointly
  encodes moneyness × time-to-expiry × underlying vol (a 5%-OTM contract is ~2-delta at 1DTE
  [illiquid] but ~35-delta at 6mo [liquid]; high-vol single names like MU/SNDK have wider liquid
  ranges than SPY). `WHERE delta IS NOT NULL AND abs(delta) BETWEEN x AND y` self-excludes the
  illiquid tail (delta is NULL in exactly the IV-NULL rows). Operator's insight, fully correct.
- **Research daemon: started 2026-05-21 21:11, then killed after draining the queue.** Killed
  deliberately — 53 rejected/failed experiments are MORE than enough to map the gaps. Restart
  after the codegen fix lands, not before.
